## Relevance to Book I

This is the short pre-*Principia* treatise sent in the 1684 context. The
normalized text begins with the area law under a centripetal force (Theorem 1),
then develops circular and elliptical motion, inverse-square implications,
orbital determination, and related problems. It is a direct predecessor for
Book I’s early central-force propositions and should be read alongside the
Motte 1846 *Principia* and the Motte/Wilkins Section I excerpt.

The normalized view hides 137 deletions, silently includes 99 additions, and
applies 53 editorial regularizations. The diplomatic view preserves those
revision signals and is authoritative when wording or composition matters;
the XML is the preferred machine-readable source.

## Extraction and coverage

The three files were downloaded with `curl -fL` and checked with `file` and
SHA-256. No OCR or formula repair was performed. The HTML and XML were spot
checked for the title, metadata, Theorem 1, the ellipse argument, and the
manuscript page anchors. This is a source archive and focused catalogue, not a
complete independent diplomatic or mathematical audit.
