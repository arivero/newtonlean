three respects : in the first place I have corrected the diagrams,
and all purely clerical blunders, by Newton’s own copy; in the

have altered the initial letters of words to capitals or small
letters as the case may be, so as to make the practice uniform

exactly); and in the third place I have written the symbol ×
instead of +, and instead of et, when + and et are used (as in
these manuscripts they not infrequently are) to indicate
multiplication. I have also replaced any symbol like ABC by
AB × BC when it is used to denote the product of AB and
BC. I need hardly add that in the writings of mathematicians
of the seventeenth century expressions like xq, xc, xqq, χqc stand
for what we usually write as z2, jk3, x4, x5.
