# M1 — quadratic deflection genealogy

Status: substantial checked reconstruction; historical discharge remains open.

## Evidence

The separate TEI witnesses and marked passages are indexed in passages.json and
passages.md. Definitions I, II, III, IV, V, VII and VIII, laws I and II, motion
composition, Lemma IX and the velocity-area proof of Lemma X are extracted
separately for 1687 and 1713. Identifying English translations are labelled;
some supporting passages remain Latin-only. Formula layout must be checked
against the TEI, retained verbatim in each extracted record.

Royal Society-copy **edited reprint**: Rouse Ball (1893), p.36, explicitly prints
Hypothesis 4, “Spatium quod corpus ... ipso motus initio describit, esse in
duplicata ratione temporis.” Number and wording were visually checked. The
editor declares corrections on p.35. This establishes an independent numbered
witness, not the earliest manuscript layer. NATP00089 still retains its own
Hyp 1 → Lem 2 citation correction; do not identify those layers with H4.
NATP00090 provides the separate Lemma 2 proof and Galileo attribution.

1687 says regular force; 1713 specifies finite force, immutable or continuously
increasing/decreasing. Both explicitly cite Lemma IX. Lemma IX's statement
mentions triangles, while its proof uses curvilinear areas; this textual
compression is retained, not silently repaired into a proven construction.
1713 adds Lemma X corollaries 3–5. The graph's cross-edition edges are textual
comparisons, never proof dependencies.

## Checked constructions

Lean 4.19.0, core/Std only, empty external dependencies:

- `Magnitudes` now requires positive inhabitance, irreflexive strict order,
  reflexive/transitive weak order, strict/weak compatibility and surrounding
  comparisons for every coefficient, as well as arbitrarily small intervals.
  `near_has_witness` and `not_near_false` exclude empty neighbourhood proofs.
- `Fraction.magnitudes` is a concrete signed rational model with positive
  denominators and cross-multiplication order. Representations are not reduced;
  magnitude equality is `equiv`, proved equivalent to mutual weak comparison.
  Addition/multiplication satisfy checked commutativity, associativity,
  distributivity and the order compatibility used here. This is a small
  rational consistency model, not a claim that all Newtonian lengths are rational.
- `deflectionRatio` constructs s/t²; t must be positive. `ratio` uses an explicit
  zero extension only outside positive times. `square_ratio` proves cancellation.
  `triangleArea` constructs half base times perpendicular height;
  `triangle_area_ratio` proves its normalized ratio is half the secant slope.
  `constructed_triangle_limit` derives its limit from that contact premise,
  rather than taking a whole triangle limit as a field. The doubled-area
  algebraic helper `triangle_normalized_limit` also remains available.
- `Principia1687.constructed_quadratic_bridge` uses that construction and
  enclosure to obtain the earlier positive-coefficient predicate. Contact limits,
  mechanical area enclosure and positive coefficient remain explicit premises.
- 1713 coefficient reconstructions rearrange the exact model s=k f t² with a
  shared calibration k. Corollary 4 requires k,t positive and permits zero f;
  corollary 5 requires k,f positive. These are algebra of limiting coefficients,
  NOT a finite-time law for arbitrary variable force. Comparable accelerative
  force, or motive force with matched mass, and matching directions and initial
  conditions are required for the historical interpretation.

## Exact remaining gaps

1. Direct earliest manuscript witness and insertion chronology for H4.
2. Construct the actual curved Lemma IX diagram and prove its contact limits
   from the source's nondegenerate angles and similarity. The new normalized
   triangle algebra is proved; the curved contact assertion is not.
3. Derive the mechanical velocity-area enclosure from independently formalized
   mechanical premises. Naming it as a structure field does not discharge it.
4. Prove the initial variable-force comparison corollaries from those premises;
   the current exact-coefficient rearrangements do not supply that theorem.

Constant-force impulse route (22 September 2026, modern reconstruction):
`PartitionControl`, `PartialCell`, `UniformRefinement` and
`PartitionComparison` derive from the finite end-kick recurrence (impulses at
cell ends, a modelling convention) that polygon positions approach
`p + t*v + (t²/2)*a` at every rational time. The residual is exact, below any
rational tolerance under explicit refinement, and partition-independent. This
recovers the Lemma X coefficient 1/2 for **constant** force without the
velocity-area enclosure. Gaps 3–4 remain open for variable force.

Finite enclosure under the 1713 clause (22 September 2026, modern
reconstruction): `Polygon/MonotoneEnclosure.lean` proves, for the impulse
construction with integer magnitudes and unit cells, that impulses between
`lo` and `hi` enclose the space described between the constant-force spaces.
For a force that continually increases or decreases (the 1713 wording of
Lemma X), the bounds are the initial and final forces. A finite force that
rises and falls escapes the enclosure. This is the finite mechanical side of
gap 3. The velocity-area identification for a given curve and the passage to a
continuous force remain open.

The earlier generic `lemmaX_reconstruction` remains as a logical squeeze API.
It is not a completed proof of Lemma IX or X. Zero force cannot discharge the
positive-coefficient bridge. No derivative, integration theorem, or mathlib is used.
