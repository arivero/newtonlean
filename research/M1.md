## Newtonian proof boundary

At the user's direction, no post-Newtonian theorem may supply the argument.
The project therefore uses Lean core only: no mathlib, derivatives, Taylor
expansions, measure theory, or integration theorem. The previous exact global
real-valued polynomial predicate and tautological coefficient theorem are gone.

The checked argument formalizes ordered enclosure on shrinking positive
intervals. Magnitudes and ratios are abstract. Common refinement, compatibility
of strict and weak order, geometric limits of bounding triangles, and the
velocity-area identification are explicit premises. The Lean theorem combines
these premises by order reasoning. This is named a reconstruction, never a
historical theorem or a complete proof of Lemma IX.

The conditional bridge yields a positive limiting quadratic ratio only when
positivity is explicitly supplied. Zero-force degeneracy is not hidden inside
nondegenerate proportionality. Variable-force motion is neither assumed to be
an exact polynomial nor declared covered without establishing the geometric
premises. No concrete real magnitude model is constructed in this increment.

Additional obligations relative to Newton's compressed proof:

1. Construct the ratio of deflection to the square of elapsed time from
   geometrical magnitudes; it is presently an input function.
2. Derive the triangle limits from the appropriate edition's Lemma IX and its
   cited geometry, including nondegenerate contact assumptions.
3. Justify the identification of generated displacement with velocity-diagram
   area from the mechanical premises; do not infer it from textual ordering.
4. Establish the enclosing inequalities for regular force without importing
   a modern smoothness theorem.
5. Verify a complete first-state witness before claiming that Principia
   discharges a specifically numbered De Motu hypothesis.

## Status

M1 is not complete. The evidence table, source extraction, graph validation,
and conditional enclosure proof are usable; the full historical discharge is
not established. M2–M4 remain deferred. The 1713 namespace shares the checked
enclosure argument but does not assert identical mechanical hypotheses across
editions, or formalize its force-comparison corollaries yet.
