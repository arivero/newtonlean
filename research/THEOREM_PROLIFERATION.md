# Why theorem counts do not measure proof completion

The user requested this analysis on 5 October 2026. The dated measurements
and review are preserved in Git. The Python counting/classification pipeline,
its history CSV and completion plots were removed on 6 October; this note
retains the implementation findings, not an active progress metric.

Lean core supplies neither the project's rational geometry nor its completion
library. Finite estimates, order/exhaustion, Cauchy names, representative
independence, quotient operations, time addresses and actual square covers
therefore generate many supporting declarations. A completed operation needs
finite control, a Cauchy proof, transport through equivalent names, a quotient
lift and compatibility/bound lemmas. Those steps can be necessary without
each discharging an independent Newtonian obligation.

Raw Fraction representatives retain positive denominators and use `equiv`
rather than record equality. Congruence, endpoint aliases, order compatibility
and finite force-sampling errors require explicit transport. Thin realization
and embedding interfaces also generate theorem names without new limits.

Avoidable growth came from proving harmonic and general clients separately.
The shared finite polygon, geometric-tail, calibrated recurrence, BinaryLift
and matched-region cores now reuse genuine estimates. Retained compatibility
statements and force-specific controls remain useful, with no separate proof-
completion credit. Reachability from historical files is an inspection signal,
not a reason to discard future secant/tangent groundwork or tested instances.

The former classifier examined statement syntax, not proof depth, use or
novelty. Names containing `sample` caused universal sampled-map theorems to
look like numeric controls, while realization identities could look substantive.
A change in namespace or file order could change duplicate classification
without changing mathematics. Counts and weighted percentages therefore did
not establish that an area identification, physical premise or historical
limiting argument had been proved.

Assess progress by the precise remaining implication and the theorem that
actually discharges it. Reuse supporting mechanisms; do not add an inventory,
wrapper family or new completed operation merely to raise a count. Modern
conditional fan laws and path-content controls remain distinct from Newton's
ordinary swept-sector conclusion. [STATE.md](STATE.md) records these boundaries
and [the handoff](HANDOFF-2026-10-06-REWORKED-SOURCES.md) sets the proof order.
