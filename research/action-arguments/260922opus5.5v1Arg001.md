# Arg001 v1: the checked refinement residuals vanish with the mesh

- Proposer: Claude Opus 5.5 (Claude Code), 22 September 2026.
- Verdict: **against** a nonzero action constant arising from the refinement
  routes checked so far. Other routes remain open.
- Layer: action diagnostic (GOALS.md). It adds no historical dependency edge.

## Claim

A finite construction that produced a nonzero action would need a residual
that stays bounded away from zero as the cells shrink. Every residual checked
in this repository shrinks at least linearly in the largest cell duration. The
premises also admit unlimited refinement. Hence these routes select no action
floor, and inserting one would add a premise foreign to the constructions.

## Steps and status

1. **Zero force, exact agreement** (`modern_reconstruction`, checked).
   `ZeroForce.partitionMotion_zero_force` and
   `ZeroForce.partitionMotion_cross_partition` show that every finite rational
   schedule reaches `p + t*v` exactly, independently of partition and
   denominator. `InertialDefect.inertialWalk_closed` gives zero signed doubled
   area for every closed inertial walk. Every residual is zero at every scale,
   so any action-valued quantity formed from these residuals is identically zero.

2. **Constant force, exact residual** (`modern_reconstruction`, checked).
   `PartitionControl.candidate_partitionMotion_residual` gives the position
   residual `(Q/(2D²))*a` at schedule endpoints, and
   `PartialCell.candidate_partial_residual` gives `((Q+u*u)/(2D²))*a` inside a cell.
   Velocity residuals are exactly zero. With largest cell `δ = M/D` and
   elapsed time `t`, `residual_mesh_bound` and `partial_residual_mesh_bound`
   give coefficient `≤ δ*t/2`.

3. **Action formed from the residual** (`modern_reconstruction`; the scalar
   coefficient part is checked in `UniformRefinement.uniform_refinement_small`). Give the body mass `m`. The only
   action-dimensioned products available from the scheme are the residual
   displacement times a momentum of the motion, `m*|v + s*a|` for `s ≤ t`.
   By step 2 each is at most `(δ*t/2)*|a|*m*(|v|+t*|a|)`, which is linear in
   `δ`. `uniform_refinement_small` proves that, for every positive rational
   `ε` and rational time `N/E`, an explicit refinement reaching that time has
   residual coefficient at most `ε`. The momentum factor and mass are still
   applied by hand.

4. **Refinement is an explicit premise** (`explicit_dependency` for the
   wording; its use against an action floor is `editorial_interpretation`).
   Section I Scholium, now in the passage store: 1687
   [NATP00077 par40](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00077#par40)
   and [par42](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00077#par42),
   1713 [NATP00082 par46](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00082#par46)
   and [par48](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00082#par48).
   Quantities treated as particles are *evanescentia divisibilia*. Ultimate
   ratios are *limites ad quos quantitatum sine limite decrescentium rationes
   semper appropinquant*, and they give no *quantitates ultimæ*. The wording is
   the same in both editions apart from spelling.

5. **Homogeneous grading** (`modern_reconstruction`, unchecked). Lemma X
   (1687 par28, 1713 par29) gives quadratic deflection in time and Lemma XI
   Cor. 4–5 give cubic areas (catalogued locators `NATP00077.par37`, `NATP00082.par44`). The grading
   `h → Δp ~ h, Δq⊥ ~ h², area ~ h³` is homogeneous. A fixed positive floor
   breaks it at some scale, and nothing in Lemmas I–XI supplies such a scale.

## GOALS.md tests

| Test | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Positivity | Fails: the residual actions have infimum zero |
| Finiteness | Holds, trivially |
| Partition stability | Fails: the residual actions depend on `δ` and vanish with it |
| System independence | Not reached |
| Action-rescaling freedom | Every candidate scales with the chosen mesh, so the mesh is the only scale |

## What would overturn this verdict

- A same-stage finite construction for a **varying** central force whose
  realization residual has a positive lower bound independent of the mesh.
  The general central-force realization (TASKS.md order 4) is still open.
- A proof that some required ultimate ratio fails to exist for an admissible
  force, stated with its premises. Failure of a Lean tactic would not count.
