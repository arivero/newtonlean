# Arg002 v1: the inverse-cube circular family fixes one areal velocity

- Proposer: Claude Opus 5.5 (Claude Code), 22 September 2026.
- Verdict: **inconclusive; fails system independence.** Among power-law
  families this is the one place where Proposition IV's ratios fix an
  action-valued quantity. The value depends on the force strength, so it is a
  constant of the system, and a universal one does not follow.
- Layer: action diagnostic (GOALS.md). It adds no historical dependency edge.

## Steps and status

1. **Areal velocity along the family** (`explicit_dependency` on Cor. 7 for
   the exponents; the product is `modern_reconstruction`). For uniform
   circles with `T ∝ Rⁿ`, Cor. 7 gives `v ∝ R^(1-n)`. The areal velocity
   `R*v/2`, which is Proposition I's area-per-time constant, therefore
   scales as `R^(2-n)`.

2. **Uniqueness of the invariant case** (`modern_reconstruction`; two-circle
   form checked in `Diagnostic/InverseCubeAreal.lean`). `R^(2-n)` is constant across radii iff `n = 2`, and by Cor. 7
   that is iff `F ∝ 1/R³`. For every other power law the circular family runs
   through all positive areal velocities, and hence all positive angular
   momenta `m*R*v`. Proposition IV thus gives families with **no** least
   action for every power law except the inverse cube.

3. **Value of the invariant** (`modern_reconstruction`, calibrated units).
   Write `F = k/R³` and calibrate Cor. 1 as `F = m*v²/R`, a modern absolute
   form of Newton's proportion. Then `m*v²*R² = k`, so every circular orbit
   in the family has the same angular momentum `m*R*v = √(m*k)`. The same
   dimensional count holds for general power laws: `F = k*Rᵖ` combines with
   `m` into an action `m^(1/2)*k^(1/2)` exactly when `p = -3`.

4. **Similarity** (`modern_reconstruction`). For `F ∝ Rᵖ`, the mechanical
   similarity `R → λR`, `t → λ^((1-p)/2) t` (the content of Cor. 7 read as a
   scaling) multiplies action by `λ^((3+p)/2)`. Only `p = -3` preserves
   action. Every other force law carries an action-rescaling freedom that
   forbids any fixed action floor internal to the law.

## Assessment

This is a classical precedent for a Newtonian force law fixing an
action-valued magnitude, recovered from a 1713 corollary inside the approved
Proposition IV target. It also shows why inverse-square gravity cannot supply
one: its similarity rescales action. With only `G` and masses, no action can
be formed at all (`G*M*m²` has dimensions `M²L³T⁻²`), and `G*m²/c` needs the
post-Newtonian constant `c`. Identifying `√(m*k)` with Planck's constant
would need a further physical bridge, one that also explains why a single
value should hold for every system. Nothing in the sources supplies that bridge.

Modern physics note (context only, not used as a premise): the classical
inverse-cube problem is the scale-invariant `1/r²` potential, where
`L² = m*k` marks the classical fall-to-centre threshold.
