# Arg004 v1: the revisions let magnitudes vanish and keep action finite

- Proposer: Claude Opus 5.5 (Claude Code), 22 September 2026.
- Verdict: **supports the kind of constant; value undetermined.** From
  De Motu to 1713 the drift–impulse construction stays fixed. The revisions
  move the limit language from moments to divisible particles and ultimate
  ratios, and 1713 adds a corollary stating the construction's conserved
  product of velocity and perpendicular. The quantities the construction
  conserves exactly, at every mesh and for every force of its class, all have
  the dimension of action. A universal minimum compatible with Newton's
  revised method must therefore be an action.
- Layer: action diagnostic (GOALS.md). It adds no historical proof edge.

The cell is the same in all three: the Law I continuation *recta pergeret ad
c*, the impulse *impulsu unico sed magno*, and the composition that places
the body at C. Only its citations move. The De Motu margins read *Hyp. 1*
(NATP00089) and *[del: Hypoth.] [add: Lex] 1* (NATP00090); 1687 and 1713 cite
*Leg. I* and *Legum Corol. 1*.

The Scholium closing Section I glosses the new word (1687
[par40](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00077#par40),
1713 [par46](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00082#par46)):
*siquando quantitates tanquam ex particulis constantes consideravero … nolim
indivisibilia sed evanescentia divisibilia, non summas & rationes partium
determinatarum, sed summarum & rationum limites semper intelligi*. Its stated
reason is method: *durior est indivisibilium Hypothesis; & propterea Methodus
illa minus Geometrica censetur*. It adds that the two methods reach the same
results, *His enim idem præstatur quod per methodum indivisibilium*. It answers
the objection from ultimate magnitudes (1687
[par42](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00077#par42),
1713 [par48](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00082#par48)):
*Ultimæ rationes illæ quibuscum quantitates evanescunt, revera non sunt rationes
quantitatum ultimarum, sed limites …*.

1713 also rebuilds Proposition I's corollaries. The two 1687 corollaries
([par46–47](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00077#par46):
the area law as a test of the force's direction) move to Proposition II
([1713 par61–62](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00082#par61),
extended to resisting media and to retarded description). The freed first
place receives
([1713 par52](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00082#par52),
kept in 1726 NATP00087 par50):

> *Corol. 1. Velocitas corporis in centrum immobile attracti est in spatiis non
> resistentibus reciproce ut perpendiculum a centro illo in Orbis tangentem
> rectilineam demissum. Est enim velocitas in locis illis A, B, C, D, E, ut sunt
> bases æqualium triangulorum AB, BC, CD, DE, EF; & hæ bases sunt reciproce ut
> perpendicula in ipsas demissa.*

Its reason is finite: equal triangles on the bases AB, BC, … make velocity
times perpendicular the same at every vertex. Corollaries 3–4
([par54–55](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00082#par54))
then measure forces by diagonals and sagittae *ubi arcus isti in infinitum
diminuuntur*.

## Steps and status

1. **Fixed finite core** (`explicit_dependency`, all stages): the
   drift–impulse cell.
2. **Limit language revised** (explicit text; the reading is
   `editorial_interpretation`). Moments that each carry one triangle become
   divisible particles, and the finite content moves from magnitudes to
   ultimate ratios.
3. **The ratio made explicit in 1713** (explicit; checked in finite form).
   Cor. 1 states that velocity × perpendicular is constant. Times the mass it
   is an angular momentum, with the dimension of action.
   `CentralSchedule.cell_momentum` and `schedule_momentum` prove its exact
   invariance at every mesh, for unequal cells and any central field.
4. **Phase area** (`modern_reconstruction`; checked in the phase plane). The
   cell is a drift followed by an impulse, and each moves one coordinate by an
   amount that depends only on the other.
   - `PhaseArea.drift_rigid` and `kick_rigid` show, for every field, that
     lines of fixed velocity (drift) and lines of fixed position (impulse) are
     carried rigidly. That is Cavalieri's criterion for equal areas.
   - For affine fields (constant force; force as distance), `cell_area2` and
     `cells_area2` prove that every phase triangle keeps its doubled area at
     every cell duration.
   - `refinement_moves_points_keeps_area`: one cell and two half cells send a
     point to different places, and both keep the area.

   Phase area per unit mass has the dimension of action.
5. **Energy-type invariants depend on the mesh** (checked). The constant-force
   cell conserves `v² − 2aq − d·a·v` exactly (`cell_energyD`), and that
   expression changes with `d` (`energyD_depends_on_mesh`). The harmonic
   invariant of `HarmonicStability` also contains `d`.
6. **Inference** (`editorial_interpretation`). A universal minimum must
   survive refinement of the cells and hold for every force. Lengths, times
   and configuration areas are the Scholium's evanescent divisible magnitudes.
   Energy-type invariants change with the mesh. The quantities that meet both
   requirements have the dimension of action: phase area (exact for affine
   fields; for every field by Cavalieri's principle applied to the checked
   rigid shears, which is not yet formalized) and the areal product (every
   central field, checked). Newton's argument against
   least magnitudes (par42: indivisibles would contradict Euclid X on
   incommensurables) concerns single magnitudes. A phase cell of fixed area
   can be arbitrarily thin in position or in velocity, so a bound on it leaves
   the Euclidean argument intact.

## Modern context (never a premise)

In Planck's 1906 lectures on heat radiation, h is the area of the elementary
region of an oscillator's phase plane, and the old quantum theory quantized
`∮p dq` in units of h. Both attach to the invariant of step 4. Bohr's
quantization of angular momentum attaches to that of step 3.
