# Primary source inventory for M1

## 1684 *De motu corporum in gyrum*

- Isaac Newton, *De motu corporum in gyrum*, late 1684, Cambridge University
  Library MS Add. 3965.7, ff. 55–62*, Newton Project NATP00089.
  - [normalized transcription](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00089)
  - [diplomatic transcription](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00089)
  - [TEI/XML](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/xml/NATP00089)
  - Local copies: `docs/Newton_DeMotuCorporumInGyrum_NATP00089_{normalized,diplomatic}.html`
    and `.xml`.
  - Identifying passage: Hypothesis 3 says a body carried by combined forces
    proceeds as it would under the forces successively; the opening Theorem 1
    constructs equal areas in equal times by polygonal impulses. The local
    transcription is Latin; the exact quadratic-deflection wording requires a
    focused passage extraction rather than an inferred translation.

## *Principia* 1687 opening and Book I

- Isaac Newton, *Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica*, first edition,
  1687. The project currently has a reliable English witness for the opening
  material and Section I excerpt in the sibling research archive:
  - [`../navstokgap/docs/Newton_Principia_Motte1846.html`](../../navstokgap/docs/Newton_Principia_Motte1846.html),
    Motte/Chittenden 1846, selected opening material and Book I propositions.
  - [`../navstokgap/docs/Newton_Principia_BookI_SectionI_Motte1729_Wilkins2002.pdf`](../../navstokgap/docs/Newton_Principia_BookI_SectionI_Motte1729_Wilkins2002.pdf),
    Motte 1729 transcription edited by Wilkins, Book I Section I.
  - These are not 1687 witnesses and are indexed only as reading aids until a
    Latin first-edition source is archived.

## 1713 comparison witness

- Newton Project, *De Motu Corporum (Liber Primus) (1713)*, NATP00082:
  [diplomatic](https://newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00082),
  [normalized](https://newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00082).
  This is the primary online witness to use for the 1713 quadratic-motion
  comparison; it has not yet been archived locally in M1.
